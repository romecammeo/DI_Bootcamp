- Write three actionable productivity tips, connecting them naturally to FlowNest where supported by the product information.
- Use a friendly, professional tone suited to LinkedIn.
- Keep the post concise, with short paragraphs and a relevant call to action.
- Return the finished post in a copyable Markdown code block.

For future productivity-tool promotions, reuse this general structure while adapting the product details, audience, and claims to the information provided.

### prompt 3

Act as a financial analyst preparing a three-minute briefing for a non-technical executive team.

Summarize the attached monthly financial report, prioritizing the three most decision-relevant findings.

For each finding:
- Explain the financial significance in clear, concise language.
- Include supporting figures and reporting periods when available in the report.
- Highlight meaningful changes, risks or trends supported by the source.

Use a professional tone and a structured briefing format with headings and bullet points. Target approximately 350–400 spoken words.

Use only information supported by the attached report. Do not invent figures, causes or financial conclusions. Explicitly note material information that is unavailable.

Make the briefing suitable for presenting aloud in approximately three minutes.
